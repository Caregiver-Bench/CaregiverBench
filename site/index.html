CaregiverBench

An open benchmark for how well AI models answer the questions dementia caregivers actually ask.

Pre-alpha · 30 draft items · clinical validation in progress

More than eleven million Americans provide unpaid care for someone living with dementia, and a growing share of them turn to AI chatbots at 2 a.m. when no nurse line is open. Their questions mix medical facts, behavioral strategy, safety, ethics, and their own exhaustion. A response that sounds confident can still be wrong in ways that matter: recommending reality orientation for someone who asks to "go home," suggesting an antihistamine for sleep, or missing the signal that a caregiver is in crisis.

No public, clinically grounded benchmark measures this. General medical QA benchmarks test recall of textbook facts; they do not test whether a model gives a frightened spouse advice a dementia specialist would endorse. CaregiverBench is meant to fill that gap.

How it works

Realistic questionsWritten the way caregivers actually ask them, with a persona: spouse or adult child, same house or long distance, early or late stage.
Rubrics, not answer keysEach item lists what a sound answer must include, must not include, and should include. Models are scored on the rubric, so there is no single right wording.
Safety firstA single must-not violation zeroes the item. Crisis items check whether a model addresses risk before anything else.
Clinical validationEvery item shows its review status. Only items validated by two independent clinicians count toward the headline score.

Ten categories

  • Communication
  • Behavioral symptoms
  • Daily care
  • Safety
  • Medical
  • Stages and prognosis
  • Caregiver wellbeing
  • Legal and financial
  • Care transitions
  • End of life

Get involved

We especially need dementia-care clinicians, social workers, and experienced caregivers to review items. Reviewers use a simple web tool; no technical background or GitHub account is needed. Reviewers are credited by name unless they prefer otherwise.

Open the review tool Source and data on GitHub

CaregiverBench is a research instrument for evaluating AI systems. Nothing in it is medical advice, and it must not be used to guide the care of any individual.
