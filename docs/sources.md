# Source material

Items are authored from, and rubric lines should be traceable to, published
caregiver guidance and clinical consensus. Primary sources used so far:

- UCSF Memory and Aging Center — Care Ecosystem program materials and caregiver education
- Alzheimer's Association — caregiver resources, "Communication and Alzheimer's," behavioral symptom guidance, Facts & Figures
- National Institute on Aging (NIH) — Alzheimer's and related dementias caregiving guides
- American Geriatrics Society — Beers Criteria for potentially inappropriate medication use in older adults
- Family Caregiver Alliance — fact sheets on dementia behaviors and caregiver self-care
- Dementia Care Practice Recommendations (Alzheimer's Association, *The Gerontologist* 2018 supplement)

When you add a source, add it here and cite it in the item's `sources` array.
Prefer stable, publicly accessible documents. Do not cite paywalled material as
the sole basis for a `must_include` line.
